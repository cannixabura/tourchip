**完整說明**：
帶團最怕有人走散。旅行團是給領隊、導遊、老師和社團幹部用的帶團工具：開一個團，團員掃一下 QR code 就入團，不用加好友、不用留電話。

【集合，每支手機都會響】
在地圖上把光柱拖到集合點、選好時間，全團手機立刻響起專屬提示音，連專注模式都擋不住。沒按「收到」的人每分鐘再提醒一次，最多 3 次；也可以只叫某一組，或單獨呼叫一個人。

【集合點是一道光柱】
團員打開 App，3D 夜光地圖會跟著手機轉，集合點就是立在建築之間的一道光柱。卡片上有當地時間、倒數、距離和方向，抬頭就知道往哪走。

【點名】
團員回「我到了」「還要 5 分鐘」「需要幫忙」，需要幫忙的人排在最上面，領隊和共同團主都會收到提醒。

【和團員互相看得到】
同意分享後，同一團的人會在 3D 地圖上看到彼此的大頭貼，還能發氣泡（例如「在找廁所」「買到珍奶了」）。長按大頭貼就能傳訊息，對方立刻收到通知。只有同團的人看得到，不在團裡的人看不到任何人；隨時可以在設定關掉，退團或團解散就刪除。

【我迷路了】
按一下，就把你現在的位置傳給領隊，地圖上出現紅點和你的團號，30 分鐘後自動刪除。

【公告與行程表】
公告可以發給全團或某一組；行程表的時間一律用集合點的當地時間，跨國行程也不會算錯。

【安心】
・不需要註冊帳號，不收集電話、Email 或真實姓名
・訊息有不當字詞過濾，可以封鎖、檢舉
・未滿 18 歲的團員要先取得家長或監護人同意才能分享位置與傳訊息
・行程最後一天 23:59 團自動解散，名單、位置、訊息全部刪除

【收費】
20 人以內免費，團員永遠免費。第 21 人起，團主可購買「7 天方案」（一次性購買，不自動續費），人數上限提高到 100 人；旅行社也可以先買團購券，變成 QR code 發給領隊使用。

## English

**App name**：TourChip: Lead Your Group

**Subtitle (App Store, 30 chars)**：Meetups, roll calls & notices

**Short description (Play, 80 chars)**：Lead a group trip: everyone's phone rings for meetups, and a 3D light shows where.

**Promotional text (App Store)**：
Never lose anyone on a group trip. TourChip turns the meeting point into a pillar of light on a 3D map, rings every phone for a meetup, and shows the leader who got the call. Free up to 20 people, always free for members.

**Keywords (App Store, 100 chars)**：
tour,leader,guide,group,meetup,roll call,school trip,travel,itinerary,lost,map,headcount

**Full description**：
Never lose anyone on a group trip. TourChip is a group tool for tour leaders, guides, teachers and club organizers: start a group, and members join by scanning a QR code. No friend requests, no phone numbers.

MEETUPS THAT EVERY PHONE HEARS
Drag the light pillar to the meeting point and pick a time. Every phone in the group plays a dedicated sound, even in Focus mode. Anyone who hasn't tapped "Got it" is reminded every minute, up to 3 times. Call one sub-group, or ping a single person.

THE MEETING POINT IS A PILLAR OF LIGHT
Members see a glowing 3D night map that turns with their phone. The meetup stands among the buildings as a pillar of light, with local time, countdown, distance and direction.

ROLL CALL
Members answer "I'm here", "5 more minutes" or "I need help". Those who need help are listed first, and leaders are alerted.

SEE YOUR GROUP ON THE MAP
After agreeing to share, people in the same group see each other's photos on the 3D map and can post bubbles. Long-press a photo to send a message with an instant notification. Only your group can see you; turn sharing off any time in Settings. Locations are deleted when you leave or the group ends.

I'M LOST
One tap sends your current location to your leaders as a red dot with your number. It is deleted after 30 minutes.

NOTICES AND ITINERARY
Post notices to everyone or a sub-group. Itinerary times always use each meetup's local time zone.

SAFETY
- No account, no phone number, email or real name
- Messages are filtered, and you can block and report
- Members under 18 need a parent or guardian's consent to share location or message
- The group ends at 23:59 on the last day and everything is deleted

PRICING
Free up to 20 people, always free for members. From the 21st member, the leader can buy a one-time 7-day pass (no subscription) to raise the limit to 100. Agencies can buy group passes in advance and hand them to leaders as QR codes.
