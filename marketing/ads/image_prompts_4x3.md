# 旅行團 TourChip 廣告插圖 第二批：20 張 4:3

和開場導覽的 10 張圖完全不同，每張只講一個 App 裡真的有的功能。
比例 **4:3 橫式**（建議 1600×1200 以上），給 FB、IG、Threads、X 貼文用。
生成好之後照編號命名 `ad43_01.png` … `ad43_20.png` 傳回來，我再疊上標題、App 圖示和 App Store 徽章（各語言版本）。

## 共同風格（每張提示詞前面都要貼上這段）

```
Cinematic digital illustration, 4:3 landscape. "Night glow" style: deep navy night (#070B1F)
with soft aurora gradients of violet (#B44CFF) and sky blue (#4FC3F7), warm street lights,
gentle bloom and glow, slightly stylized 3D look like a premium animated film still.
Asian travelers of mixed ages, friendly and natural expressions, modern casual travel clothes.
Smartphones show simplified glowing UI made of shapes and icons only.
Keep the top 30% of the frame calm and dark (sky or soft blur) for a headline to be added later.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand names, no watermarks.
```

（提示詞寫英文，生成效果通常比較好；每張後面的中文是給你看的說明。）

### 01 發集合：拖曳地圖放光柱
領隊用手指在 3D 地圖上拖出集合點，城市裡立起一道光柱。
```
Close-up of a tour leader's hand holding a smartphone in the foreground; on the screen a glowing
3D city map where a fingertip drags a meeting point. Behind the phone, the real city at night
mirrors the screen: a tall violet-blue beam of light rises between buildings at a plaza.
Small tour flag clipped to the leader's backpack strap.
```

### 02 每支手機一起響
團員分散在不同地方（咖啡店、紀念品店、寺廟前），手機同時亮起、響鈴，連鎖屏都會響。
```
Split-scene composition in one image: three travelers in three different spots of the same
old town at dusk — one in a café, one in a souvenir shop, one in front of a temple gate —
each looking at their phone which lights up at the same moment with a violet ring of glow and
small sound-wave arcs. Thin glowing lines connect the three phones to a beam of light in the
distant center.
```

### 03 全螢幕集合卡：倒數、距離、方向
手機全螢幕顯示倒數圓環和大箭頭，抬頭就看到遠處光柱。
```
Over-the-shoulder view of a young woman on a busy night street holding up her phone; the screen
shows a big circular countdown ring and a large arrow pointing forward. Far down the street,
exactly where the arrow points, a violet-blue light beam rises above the crowd. Neon reflections
on wet pavement.
```

### 04 沒按收到，再響一次・單獨呼叫
戴耳機逛街的團員沒注意，手機再次震動發光；領隊在名單上點了他一個人。
```
A teenage boy wearing big headphones browsing sneakers in a shopping arcade, unaware; his phone in
his pocket glows and pulses with a second violet ring. Inset in the upper-right corner, a leader's
phone screen shows a list of round avatars with one avatar highlighted and pulsing. Playful,
slightly comedic mood.
```

### 05 掃碼入團
機場出境大廳，領隊舉著手機顯示 QR 碼，團員們拿手機排隊掃。
```
Airport departure hall at early morning, big windows with a plane and blue dawn. A smiling tour
leader holds up a phone showing a large square QR-code pattern (abstract, glowing); five group
members of different ages lean in with their phones, each screen lighting up with a small
checkmark glow. Suitcases with matching violet luggage tags.
```

### 06 分組：1 號車、2 號車
兩台遊覽車停在一起，各自有不同顏色的光圈，團員走向自己那台。
```
Two tour coaches parked side by side at a scenic mountain rest stop at blue hour. Each coach has a
soft colored halo on the ground — one violet, one sky blue. Travelers walk toward their own coach;
their phones glow in the matching color. Clean, organized, calm feeling.
```

### 07 點名：三個答案
團員頭上浮著三種回覆的光點：我到了、再 5 分鐘、需要幫忙。
```
A tour group gathered in a lantern-lit historic street; above each member's head floats a small
glowing round icon: most show a green checkmark, two show an amber clock, one shows a red raised
hand. The leader in front looks at a phone with a tidy grid of the same colored dots.
Warm lanterns, festive but orderly.
```

### 08 需要幫忙：團主、共同團主都收到
一位團員按了需要幫忙，兩位領隊的手機同時全螢幕亮紅光。
```
An elderly woman sitting on a bench in a garden, holding her phone and pressing a red glowing
button. In the background two tour leaders in different spots of the garden both turn around as
their phones flash a full-screen soft red alert; dotted glowing lines connect her phone to both
leaders. Reassuring, caring mood, not scary.
```

### 09 我迷路了：紅點與團號
迷路的孩子在巷子裡送出位置；領隊手機地圖上出現紅點。
```
A ten-year-old girl standing at a fork of narrow alleys at night, holding a phone that sends out a
red pulsing ripple into the sky. Inset: the tour leader's phone screen showing a 3D map with one
red pulsing dot and a tiny badge on it, and the leader already walking briskly toward the alley.
Hopeful, safe feeling.
```

### 10 同一團在 3D 地圖上互相看得到
俯瞰發光的 3D 城市，團員的大頭貼標記散落在街道上。
```
High aerial view of a glowing 3D miniature city at night (stylized buildings, river, bridges).
Scattered across the streets are small round avatar markers with friendly faces, each with a soft
violet glow and a thin light stem, like pins standing on the map. One light beam stands at the
plaza. Map-like, clean, magical.
```

### 11 氣泡：在找廁所、買到珍奶了
地圖上每個人頭上有小氣泡圖示（珍奶、相機、廁所）。
```
Close, tilted view of the glowing 3D map: several avatar markers each with a small speech bubble
above them containing a single icon — a bubble-tea cup, a camera, a restroom sign shape, a ramen
bowl. Cute, light-hearted, colorful accents on the night map.
```

### 12 長按傳私訊・自動翻譯
長按地圖上的人，訊息飛過去；兩人說不同語言也看得懂。
```
A fingertip long-presses an avatar marker on a phone map; a small glowing envelope flies out of the
screen across a night market toward another traveler, whose phone receives it. Above the envelope,
two overlapping speech bubbles with different abstract script-like squiggles merge into one,
suggesting translation. Night market food stalls, steam, string lights.
```

### 13 行程表跨時區
台北和東京之間一道飛行弧線，兩個時鐘各自顯示當地時間。
```
Stylized night view from above the clouds: on the left a city skyline with a tall tower (Taipei-like,
generic), on the right a city skyline with a red lattice tower (Tokyo-like, generic), connected by a
glowing violet flight arc with a small plane. Two floating round clock faces above each city show
different times (hands only, no numbers). Itinerary cards float along the arc like a timeline.
```

### 14 每日資訊：飯店 Wi-Fi、早餐、我的房號
飯店大廳，手機上浮出 Wi-Fi、咖啡杯、房卡的圖示卡片。
```
Cozy hotel lobby at night, warm lights. A traveler checks a phone; above it float three glowing
cards: a Wi-Fi symbol, a coffee cup with a small sunrise (breakfast), and a hotel key card with a
door icon. Suitcase beside her, elevator glow in the background.
```

### 15 給司機看・帶我走過去
計程車上，團員把手機的大字地址卡給司機看。
```
Inside a taxi at night, view from the back seat: a traveler leans forward and shows the driver a
phone displaying a large clean card with a map pin icon and bold blocks (abstract, no readable
text). The friendly driver nods. City lights stream past the windows in bokeh.
```

### 16 求助卡：拿給當地人看
語言不通的長輩把求助卡拿給店家看，店家幫忙打電話給領隊。
```
A small corner shop at night in a foreign city. An elderly man traveler, a little worried, shows his
phone screen (a clean card with a phone icon and a pin icon) to a kind young shopkeeper, who is
already calling on her own phone and smiling reassuringly. Warm, kind, human moment.
```

### 17 旅行社團購券
旅行社櫃台把一疊發光的 QR 票券交給領隊們。
```
A modern travel agency office at dusk, glass windows with city lights. An agency staff member hands
a fan of glowing ticket cards, each with an abstract QR pattern, to two tour leaders holding flags.
On the desk a laptop and a world map. Professional, business-friendly mood.
```

### 18 艾婭匯入行程
艾婭（光點小精靈）把亂糟糟的紙本行程變成整齊的每日卡片。
```
A small glowing fairy-like AI assistant made of soft violet and blue light (abstract, gentle, no
detailed face) hovers above a desk. On one side, a messy pile of printed itinerary papers and
sticky notes; light streams from the fairy and transforms them into neat floating day cards with
icons (hotel bed, coffee cup, bus, clock). Tour leader watching, relieved.
```

### 19 共同團主・交棒
兩位領隊交接一支發光的團旗。
```
Two tour leaders, a middle-aged man and a young woman, at the top of stone steps at a scenic
viewpoint at blue hour, handing over a small tour flag whose tip glows violet like a torch.
Behind them the group gathers, city lights below. Trust, teamwork.
```

### 20 解散夜：送花給領隊、資料化成星光
最後一晚，團員送出發光的花給領隊；地圖上的標記化成星光消散（解散後資料刪除）。
```
Farewell night on a riverside promenade. Group members raise their phones; from each phone a small
glowing flower floats up and drifts toward the smiling tour leader. Above, the avatar markers of
the map gently dissolve into tiny stars rising into the aurora sky. Bittersweet, grateful, warm.
```
